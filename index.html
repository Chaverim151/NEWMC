About Us

Chaverim of Middlesex County, NJ is a volunteer services organization comprised of a dedicated team of volunteers. Dispatchers and service members are standing by 24 hours a day to help take care of the needs of our community.

From jump-starts and flat tires to lockouts, flooding, and everything in between. Not sure if we can help? Give us a call.
