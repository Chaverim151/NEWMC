About Us

Chaverim of Middlesex County, NJ is a volunteer services organization comprised of a dedicated team of volunteers. Dispatchers and service members are standing by 24 hours a day to help take care of the needs of our community.

From jump-starts and flat tires to lockouts, flooding, and everything in between. Not sure if we can help? Give us a call.

This Privacy Notice for Chaverim of Middlesex County describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information. Questions? Email media@chaverimmc.org.

1. What information do we collect?

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide — primarily phone numbers when you contact us for service. We do not process sensitive personal information and do not collect information from third parties. If you make a donation, payment data is handled securely by our payment processor.

2. How do we process your information?

  • To deliver and facilitate our services to you
  • To enable communication between users
  • To evaluate and improve our services and your experience

3. When and with whom do we share your information?

We may share information only in connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition of our business. We have not sold or shared any personal information to third parties in the preceding 12 months.

4. How long do we keep your information?

We retain personal information no longer than 1 year unless required by law. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymize the data.

5. How do we keep your information safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures. However, no electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Do we collect information from minors?

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe we have, please contact media@chaverimmc.org.

7. What are your privacy rights?

You may withdraw your consent to our processing of your personal information at any time by contacting us at media@chaverimmc.org.

8. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

We do not currently respond to DNT browser signals as no uniform technology standard has been finalized.

9. US residents — specific privacy rights

Residents of California, New Jersey, and other applicable states may have rights including access, correction, deletion, and opt-out of data sale. To exercise your rights, email media@chaverimmc.org.
