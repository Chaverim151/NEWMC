This Privacy Notice for Chaverim of Middlesex County describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information. Questions? Email media@chaverimmc.org.

1. What information do we collect?

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide — primarily phone numbers when you contact us for service. We do not process sensitive personal information and do not collect information from third parties.

2. How do we process your information?

  • To deliver and facilitate our services to you
  • To enable communication between users
  • To evaluate and improve our services and your experience

3. When and with whom do we share your information?

We may share information only in connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition. We have not sold or shared any personal information to third parties in the preceding 12 months.

4. How long do we keep your information?

We retain personal information no longer than 1 year unless required by law.

5. How do we keep your information safe?

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures. However, no electronic transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Do we collect information from minors?

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. Contact us at media@chaverimmc.org if you believe we have.

7. What are your privacy rights?

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at media@chaverimmc.org.

8. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

We do not currently respond to DNT browser signals as no uniform standard has been finalized.

9. US residents — specific privacy rights
